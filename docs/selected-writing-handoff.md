## Future changes

- Read the article in full and assess its voice, substance, distinct perspective, useful sources and contribution to the overall mix.
- Check the visible byline and structured author metadata on the current live page. Ownership of a publication does not establish authorship. Do not include desk, editorial-team or guest-contributor work as personal writing.
- Preserve the actual publication date, current title and original canonical URL. Check the destination resolves successfully.
- Prefer durable analysis and personal observation over expired calls to action, transient news, repetitive coverage or unresolved unsupported claims. Keep the selected set small, normally six to eight pieces.
- Avoid duplicating a piece already represented by a full essay on the personal site unless there is a clear reason.
- Use a neutral, concise summary. Do not transplant time-sensitive figures or claims of personal experience into the card. Keep publication and topic labels visible.
- Retain outbound links in new tabs with `noopener noreferrer`. The originals remain on their publishing sites. Do not create duplicate article pages or change the personal essay canonicals.
- Update the registry timestamp after a real editorial check. There is no automatic refresh or new factual approval implied by the list.

This selection verified live authorship and metadata and assessed editorial suitability. It did not run paid factual reviews, reopen the parked DARE archive audit or certify every historical claim in the source articles. No source publication or byline was changed.
