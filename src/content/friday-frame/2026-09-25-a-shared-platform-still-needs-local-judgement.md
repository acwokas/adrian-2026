A business can speak the local language and still misunderstand the customer.

This week, a coalition including AI Singapore announced a commitment to make AI more useful in languages that today’s systems serve poorly. Its scope includes voice, dialect and cultural context, recognising how much meaning can change beyond the words themselves. [Source](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/ideas/media-center/press-releases/2026/09/ai-language-partnership)

That has relevance well beyond AI.

Working across markets, I have seen how a shared commercial approach can give teams a stronger starting point. Clear positioning, consistent standards and reusable processes make expansion easier. They also give people more time to focus on customers.

But the way someone buys, the reassurance they need and the partners they trust can differ considerably between markets. A proposition that feels straightforward in one place may need a different explanation, channel or commercial model somewhere else.

Leading across regions means deciding where consistency helps and where adaptation improves the outcome.

Three questions help:

- **What should stay consistent?** The promise we make, our quality standards and the responsibilities we accept.
- **What should local teams be able to adapt?** The route to market, customer conversation and partnerships, within clear commercial boundaries.
- **Who can make those decisions?** People need defined authority and a straightforward way to resolve exceptions.

That third question often determines whether the first two are useful.

Asking a local team to deliver growth while sending every meaningful choice back to headquarters creates avoidable delays. Giving that team clear boundaries, visibility of the commercial consequences and room to act makes its local knowledge useful to the whole business.

From Singapore, working across Southeast Asia and beyond, I want to build on what we can share while staying curious about what needs to change.

A common platform gives us a foundation. Local judgement helps us put it to work.
