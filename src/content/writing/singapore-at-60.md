Originally published on LinkedIn Pulse, 8 August 2025, written for Singapore's 60th birthday.

Most places scale through brute force. Singapore iterates with intent. Where other nations wrestle with data privacy as an afterthought, it builds trust into the foundation. Where most businesses chase trends, it moves on signals. After nearly 15 years living here, I cannot read the place as a city-state anymore. It reads as an operating model, and a quietly radical one.

The essay walks through what I think Singapore gets right that companies routinely get wrong, and what the next 60 years of the experiment might ask of the operators who live and build here.
